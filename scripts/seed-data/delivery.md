# Ridgeline Trailer Co. - Sample Pickup and Delivery Policy

This is an illustrative demo delivery policy for our Austin, Texas dealership.
Pickup is available during posted store hours after the sale is completed.
Bring a suitable tow vehicle, the correct hitch and ball, working trailer wiring
and the identification or documents requested by the sales team.

Local delivery may be available by prior arrangement. The delivery fee depends on
distance and trailer size and is shown separately on your quote. Do not assume delivery
is free. Confirm the address, access and a receiving contact before scheduling.

We do not promise same-day delivery. Contact the team at (512) 555-0148 for the current
schedule. Inspect the trailer at handover and report concerns promptly.
