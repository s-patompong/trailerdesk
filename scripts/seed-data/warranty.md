# Ridgeline Trailer Co. - Sample Warranty Policy

This is an illustrative demo policy, not a manufacturer's warranty certificate.
New trailers are supplied with the manufacturer's applicable limited warranty.
Warranty length and covered components vary by manufacturer and model. Ask our team
for the written warranty for the specific trailer before purchase.

Keep your bill of sale and VIN. Contact the dealership at (512) 555-0148 before
arranging warranty work. We will help identify the manufacturer and the right service
process. Do not authorize repairs expecting reimbursement before the manufacturer approves.

Normal wear items, tires, damage from overloading, misuse and unauthorized changes
may be excluded by the manufacturer's terms. Used trailers are sold with the terms
listed on their bill of sale; do not assume a new-trailer warranty applies.
