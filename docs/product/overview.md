# TrailerDesk

TrailerDesk helps a trailer dealership keep track of its lot, shoppers and sales.
A salesperson can find a trailer, follow up with a customer, reserve it, prepare a
quote and finish the sale. Owners and managers can see which trailers need attention
and keep the team organized. Shoppers can browse available trailers and contact the
store through the dealership's public website.

Each dealership has its own records. A staff member working at more than one store
chooses which dealership to work in. Owners control store settings and staff access.
Salespeople can work with customers without seeing dealer costs or profit margins.

The app uses US dollars, US date formats and the store's local time. Email messages
stay in a local mail viewer during development. Real customer emails are enabled
only when production email is configured.

This is the product scope. See the Phase 1 tracker for what has actually been verified.
